अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले महत्वपूर्ण प्रश्न

You can contact our college directly by telephone at +91 9219707070, via WhatsApp for swift student advisory, by email at info@cgsmahavidyalaya.org, or by submitting the online enquiry form provided on this page. Our central office at Manikpur, Sadabad is open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Chaudhary Gulab Singh Mahavidyalaya is situated in Manikpur, Sadabad, District Hathras, Uttar Pradesh (Pin Code: 281306). It is conveniently accessible from the Agra-Hathras highway corridor with abundant road public transport connectivity and local commute links.

Admissions for undergraduate and allied faculties follow state university regulations and UP Higher Education Department directives. You can initiate your enquiry through our WhatsApp desk (+91 9219707070), select "Admission Information" in the enquiry form above, or visit the college administrative room with your prior academic transcripts.

CGS Mahavidyalaya offers accredited degree curriculums across Humanities, Sciences, and allied disciplines. Full syllabus frameworks, subject combinations, eligibility criteria, and fee schedules can be obtained by contacting the academic counter or requesting an institutional prospectus online.

The institution provides expansive, well-ventilated lecture rooms, departmental laboratories, a central library with reference volumes, seminar spaces, dedicated girl-student amenities, and sports fields. Visit our campus during working hours or explore our dedicated Photo Gallery section to preview our infrastructure.
